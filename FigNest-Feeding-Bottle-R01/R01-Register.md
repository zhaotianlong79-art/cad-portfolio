# FigNest R01 drawing register

**Baseline 2026-11-09. Portfolio concept for geometry review. Millimetres.**

The supplied brief is the sole design input. There are no received neck drawings,
photos, material batch data, flow targets, or moulder rules.

| ID | Drawing or model | R01 scope | Deferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| FN-001 | Assembly and section | Compare the one-piece teat and interchangeable storage cap on the same nominal glass neck | Actual glass, screw engagement, seal compression, retention |
| FN-101 | Teat blank and flow variants | Hollow revolved teat, integrated collar, rounded pull tab, fig-leaf relief, two post-pierced bore examples | Consultant shape review, qualified flow and piercing process |
| FN-102 | Fridge cap | Solid flat lid, integrated skirt, ring seal bead, tab and relief | Seal preload, mould design, leak test |
| FN-900 | Interface gauge | Smooth glass neck envelope, mouth and lip reference | Measured thread form, pitch, starts, finish, runout and tolerances |

**Shared coordinates.** Z is the bottle axis, +Z points toward the teat, and Z=0
is the nominal top of the glass sealing lip. +X locates both pull tabs.
Dimensions target undeformed model geometry. Assemblies present nominal tangent
contact, with no modeled seal compression. The interface has no thread geometry.

**Release inputs.** Stock bottle manufacturer drawing and measured samples,
brand and consultant shape feedback, flow and leak criteria, compound-specific
material and moulding rules, and the applicable test plan from the lab.
